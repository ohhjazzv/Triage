## How to add an entry

Copy the block below. Keep it short and true. If the forecast was wrong, write that down: it is the most useful thing in this file.

```
### <date> · <exam> · <who: me / a classmate (with their OK)>
- Chapters and time I had:
- What Triage told me to study:
- What it told me to leave, and whether I agreed:
- Closed-book checks: what surprised me:
- Forecast (the range it showed):
- Real marks (add when they come):
- One thing that was wrong or annoying:
```
